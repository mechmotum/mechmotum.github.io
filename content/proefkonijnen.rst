Bike Lab on the Kid's Science Show: Proefkonijnen
=================================================

Around the beginning of 2025 we were approached by TU Delft communications with
a request to participate in a Dutch children's television show about science
called Proefkonijnen_ (EN: crash test dummies). We were told that a kid had
submitted a question asking if it was possible to do a wheelie on a tandem
bicycle and the show was wondering if it is possible and if so can we help them
answer this question.

I proposed it to the lab and enough members were excited to participate, so we
agreed to help. Our job was to prove you can do a wheelie on a tandem, explain
how it works on camera, and support the two zany hosts attempting the wheelie
at TU Delft. This was the first time that we did a large lab-wide project
together like this and it ended up being a great idea, both for the fun and for
our own team building.

After some brainstorming, we settled on adding a counterbalance to tandem to
facilitate the wheelie and side wheels to prevent falling to the left or right.
The show's science focused on explanations about gravity, balance, levers, etc.
Bart volunteered to be our speaking representative in the show and everyone
else that helped was about to be a cheerleader in the show's wheelie scene.

It wasn't trivial to wheelie the final design, but with some coordination and
practice you could manage a wheelie. Here is Bart and one of the shop employees
