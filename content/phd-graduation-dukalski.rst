Bike Lab PhD candidate, Rado Dukalski, defended his PhD dissertation entitled
"Pursuing Points of View in Road Cycling", found here:

https://repository.tudelft.nl/record/uuid:c4e19354-0d80-4e1e-89c0-00c81723a00b

His graduation closes off the NWO funded Citius Altius Sanius P9 project. We at
the Bicycle Lab are very proud of Rado and wish him the best in his new
position at Leiden University! He always brought a jovial presence to the lab
and we will miss him.
