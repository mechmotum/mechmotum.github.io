===================
Veluwe Bicycle Tour
===================

:date: 2026-09-17 10:55:00
:tags: bicycle, social
:category: news
:authors: Jason K. Moore
:thumbnail: https://mechmotum.s3.us-east-005.dream.io/veluwe-tour-wet-forest-trails.png

We completed the fourth annual bicycle tour July 24-26; this time from Delft
through the Veluwe region ending in Nunspeet. Our largest group ever: 13
bicyclists! We covered 222 km, camping by Houten the first night, and by
Apeldoorn the second night. Haye selected a route that maximized time in the
forested areas of the Veluwe. It is really a beautiful area of the country and
we got to climb and descend a few nice 100m high hills! We had two tire
failures that required new tire purchases along the way and rain caused a slump
in morale for a bit on the final day. We ended with a view of the Veluwe from
the Nunspeet viewing tower.
