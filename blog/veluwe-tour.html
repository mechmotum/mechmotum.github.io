Veluwe Bicycle Tour

  • Thu 17 September 2026
  • news
  • Jason K. Moore
  • #bicycle, #social

We completed the fourth annual bicycle tour July 24-26; this time from Delft through the Veluwe region ending in Nunspeet. Our largest group ever: 13 bicyclists! We covered 222 km, camping by Houten the first night, and by Apeldoorn the second night. Haye selected a route that maximized time in the forested areas of the Veluwe. It is really a beautiful area of the country and we got to climb and descend a few nice 100m high hills! We had two tire failures that required new tire purchases along the way and rain caused a slump in morale for a bit on the final day. We ended with a view of the Veluwe from the Nunspeet viewing tower.
